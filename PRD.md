# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Overview
Dastiyab Store is an e-commerce platform targeted at the Pakistani market, specializing in premium tech accessories, gadgets, and personalized gifts. The platform emphasizes high-quality products, fast delivery (including Cash on Delivery in Karachi), and a seamless user experience.

## Target Audience
- Tech-savvy consumers looking for mobile accessories, earbuds, smartwatches, and laptop stands.
- Gift shoppers looking for personalized gift baskets and bouquets.
- Users who value convenience (Cash on Delivery) and trust (Easy Returns).

## Core Features
1. **Product Catalog & Search**: Advanced, fast live-search for products across multiple categories.
2. **Shopping Cart & Wishlist**: Persistent cart and wishlist functionality.
3. **Checkout System**: Manual order processing with Cash on Delivery support.
4. **Admin Dashboard**: Comprehensive admin panel for managing products, categories, orders, customers, and site settings.
5. **Dynamic Settings**: Admins can customize the top banner, stats strip, footer links, contact info, and delivery areas directly from the dashboard.
6. **AI Chatbot**: Integrated Gemini-based AI assistant for customer support.
7. **Performance & Accessibility**: Optimized images (Cloudinary), font loading, and high accessibility standards.

## Future Roadmap
- Integrated payment gateways (e.g., JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Stripe).
- User authentication and customer profiles.
- Advanced analytics and sales reporting.
- Order tracking system for customers.
